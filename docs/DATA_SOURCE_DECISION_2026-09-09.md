# Source decision — corrected 2026-09-10

This replaces the v0.1 preliminary source/budget proposal. The user requires an entirely free core; the old €50/month assumption is invalid.

Implemented source: StatsBomb Euro 2024, pinned revision, research-only derived analysis under its custom agreement. Raw data is downloaded reproducibly into a private cache and is not redistributed. Official attribution/logo are visible. No commercial operational permission is asserted.

SkillCorner has been researched but is not ingested. Metrica is deferred until the intended reuse rights are clear. Optional API-Football, Sportmonks and football-data.org adapters are not called by the real-data product and do not establish coverage.

See [the current source inventory](../DATA_SOURCES.md) for official links, exact dataset counts and distribution boundaries. Do not use this historical filename as evidence of a paid-data approval.
